## Description

**Focus on what truly matters.**

Dayfocus helps you plan your day with intention using timeboxing — a proven productivity technique used by Elon Musk, Bill Gates, and Cal Newport.

**HOW IT WORKS**

1. Set your top 3 priorities for the day
2. Drag them onto your timeline to create time blocks
3. Focus on one thing at a time
4. Complete your day with intention

**KEY FEATURES**

• **3 Daily Priorities** — Focus on what matters most
• **Visual Timeline** — See your entire day at a glance
• **Drag & Drop** — Easily schedule priorities as time blocks
• **Smart Notifications** — Get reminded when blocks start
• **Brain Dump** — Capture thoughts without cluttering your priorities
• **Dark Mode** — Easy on the eyes, day or night
• **Cloud Sync** — Access your plans across all devices (Premium)
• **Unlimited History** — Review past days anytime (Premium)

**WHY TIMEBOXING?**

Unlike endless to-do lists, timeboxing forces you to be realistic about your time. When you assign a specific time slot to a task, you're more likely to complete it.

**PREMIUM**

Free users get 3 priorities and 3 time blocks per day with 7 days of history. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited usage and cloud sync.

**PRIVACY FIRST**

Your data stays on your device by default. Sign in only if you want cloud sync — we never sell your data.
